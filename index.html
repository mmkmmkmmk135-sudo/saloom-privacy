SALOOM

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 September 2026
This policy explains how Saloom handles personal and trip data, including driver trip data received through Google Maps or Waze Navigation Connect.

1. Who we are

Saloom is a student transport coordination platform designed to connect drivers, students, guardians and transport administrators. This Privacy Policy applies to the Saloom mobile applications and related services.

2. Information we process

Depending on how Saloom is used, we may process:

  • account and profile information for drivers, guardians, students and administrators;
  • driver and vehicle information needed to operate transport services;
  • trip, stop, route, attendance, pickup, drop-off and operational event information;
  • device and notification information needed to provide app notifications;
  • precise or approximate location data when location features are enabled and required for an active transport trip;
  • support, incident and safety information submitted through Saloom.

3. Driver location and Navigation Connect data

When a driver chooses or is prompted to navigate using Google Maps or Waze through Navigation Connect, and the driver grants the required consent, Saloom may receive trip data from the navigation service. This can include the driver’s current location, trip status, estimated arrival time, remaining travel time, remaining distance and related navigation information.

Saloom uses this information to calculate and display live ETA information, coordinate the next transport stop, improve trip operations, provide relevant guardian notifications, support safety and operational oversight, and detect stale or materially changed navigation information.

Saloom does not use Navigation Connect trip data for behavioral advertising, and does not sell this trip data.

4. Parent and guardian live tracking

During an active transport trip, authorized guardians may receive live or near-live information about the vehicle serving their own child, including location, trip status and estimated arrival information. Saloom is designed to limit guardian access to the child or transport relationship they are authorized to view.

5. How we use information

  • to create, operate and manage transport trips;
  • to provide live tracking, ETA and approach notifications;
  • to enable navigation and stop-to-stop driver workflows;
  • to handle attendance, incidents, cancellations, replacements and emergency operations;
  • to secure accounts, prevent misuse and maintain audit records;
  • to diagnose technical problems and improve reliability;
  • to comply with applicable legal, safety or regulatory obligations.

6. Service providers

Saloom may use trusted service providers to deliver parts of the service. These may include Google Maps Platform / Waze Navigation Connect for navigation trip data, Mapbox for map display, and Firebase for mobile notification delivery and related app infrastructure. Routing services may also be used to calculate baseline routes and travel estimates.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices. Saloom shares only information reasonably required to provide the relevant feature.

7. Data sharing

Saloom does not sell personal information. We may share information only when needed to operate the service, with authorized users who need the information for the transport relationship, with service providers acting on our behalf, or where disclosure is required for safety, legal compliance or the protection of users and the service.

8. Data retention

We retain personal and trip information only for as long as reasonably necessary for transport operations, safety, support, dispute handling, auditing, security and applicable legal or regulatory requirements. Different record types may have different retention periods. Information that is no longer reasonably required is deleted, anonymized or otherwise handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

9. Security

Saloom uses technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect personal information, including authenticated access, role-based administrative controls, separation of operational responsibilities, and measures designed to prevent unauthorized access to trip and location information. No system can guarantee absolute security, but we work to reduce risk and respond to security issues appropriately.

10. Driver choice and consent

Navigation Connect trip-data sharing is subject to the consent experience presented by Google Maps or Waze. If a driver does not grant or later withdraws the relevant permission, Saloom will not rely on Navigation Connect trip data for that navigation session and may use other available routing or ETA methods instead.

11. Your rights and choices

Depending on applicable law and your relationship with Saloom, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or raise a privacy concern. Some information may need to be retained where reasonably necessary for safety, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, contractual obligations, or legal requirements.

12. Student and child information

Saloom is designed for student transport and may therefore process information relating to minors where necessary to provide transportation, guardian visibility and safety features. Access to student information is limited according to the user’s authorized role and transport relationship. Saloom does not use student information for targeted advertising.

13. International processing

Some technology providers used by Saloom may process or store information on infrastructure located outside the user’s country. Where this occurs, we use the service for legitimate operational purposes and expect providers to apply their applicable contractual and security protections.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Saloom develops, legal requirements change, or service providers and features change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

15. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the official Saloom support or contact channel made available in the Saloom application or its official app-store listing. Before public launch, developer information is available through the official Saloom GitHub account. Please do not post sensitive personal or trip information in public GitHub issues.
