Delete your Saloom account

Parent/guardian and driver accounts can request deletion from inside the Saloom app or through this public request channel.

For security, Saloom may verify that the request comes from the account owner before completing deletion.

Option 1 — Request deletion in the app

Open Saloom, go to Account, choose Delete account, confirm your password, and submit the request. You can view the request status in the app while it is under review.

Option 2 — Request deletion without the app

Email saloom.business@gmail.com from an address you can access. Include:

  • the phone number used for your Saloom account;
  • whether you are a parent/guardian or driver;
  • the statement: “I request deletion of my Saloom account.”

Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or sensitive student information by email.

Email Saloom support

What happens after a request

Saloom reviews the request, verifies ownership where necessary, and completes account deletion when operational requirements allow it. Account access may remain available while the request is being reviewed.

Driver deletion may be delayed while an active assignment or in-progress trip still exists. This protects transport operations and student safety.

Data deleted and data that may be retained

When deletion is completed, Saloom removes or disables the account and cleans up account-specific access, sessions, device notification registrations, and related profile data where applicable.

Some transport, safety, payment, dispute, security, or audit records may be retained when reasonably necessary for legal, operational, fraud-prevention, accounting, or safety purposes. Retained records are not kept for advertising.
